How to use Maravi

Maravi creates a MusicXML and MIDI draft on your computer. Choose audio or an image of a score; listen and review the result before using it.

1. Choose audio

Drop a file into the form or record with a microphone. Listen to the preview. If the instrument is clearer on one side of a stereo file up to 3 minutes long, choose the left or right channel. This does not separate instruments still present in that channel.

2. Choose notation

The instrument choice labels the score; it does not recognize or isolate the source. For Voice, choose a lower limit to reduce low accompaniment notes; sounds in the same register may remain. Flute omits candidates below B3. Two-staff Piano splits notes by register without identifying hands.

3. Add cues if you know them

BPM and key are optional. If left blank, Maravi analyzes the first 20 seconds and shows suggestions for you to confirm before transcribing. The metronome helps you listen, but changes the transcription BPM only after “Use heard pulse”. The initial 4/4 is a default, not a detection. A fixed BPM can harm notation with rubato.

4. Transcribe

Click “Transcribe” and wait without reloading. Processing is local; there is no real progress percentage or job cancellation yet. Options such as “Legato solo” and “Phrase notation” are editorial suggestions: check them by ear. They do not apply to two-staff Piano.

PDF or score image

On the PDF / image page, upload a printed score as PDF, PNG, JPG or TIFF. Audiveris reads symbols and MuseScore exports MusicXML and MIDI; both must be installed on this computer. Current limits: 20 MB, 8 pages and 16 megapixels per image. Handwriting and unconventional notation are not the target yet. Check every page: accidentals, rests, slurs and tuplets may be wrong.

Download and review

Open MusicXML in a score editor, compare it with the original audio or image, and listen to the MIDI. Correct pitch, rhythm, meter and phrasing. The browser preview shows only a few bars and does not display two-staff Piano. Each job lives in a temporary folder shown on the result page. To keep MusicXML and MIDI, enter the path to an existing local folder and use the save-both button; Maravi creates a new subfolder without overwriting. Browser downloads remain available.

Limits and privacy

Maravi does not send your files to a remote service. Local jobs do not expire automatically yet; mind these files on a shared computer. Automatic notation is a draft, not proof of authorship or accuracy. Some technical error details and OMR warnings may still be in Portuguese.
